## What changed

`data/constants.json` gets one new constant, `FADED_MAX_DAMAGE`. The change is additive: no
existing constant is renamed, removed or retuned.

## Why

GDD "Floor 1: The Basement", Boo-zer: "Every other turn it is Faded and takes at most 1 damage per
hit." Faded is a status, and the numbers of the other statuses (`EXPOSED_MULT`, `NOODLE_MULT`,
`HUNGOVER_MULT`) are tuning constants, so the cap is one too. CLAUDE.md says "no magic numbers in
code", so task 017's damage code reads the cap from the constants instead of writing 1.

Per-enemy numbers (how much Dorm Rats steal, the turn they flee on, the Keg Slime's split point,
Noise Complaint's card count and damage) are **not** constants: they live in each enemy's
definition file in `data/enemies/`, like a move's damage, so the content lane can tune them
without a contract change (see `docs/changes/2026-10-10-017-schema.md`).

## Constants added

| Constant | Value | Unit | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FADED_MAX_DAMAGE` | 1 | damage per hit | Most damage one hit can deal to an enemy while it is Faded. Applies to every hit, attack or not: the hit's damage is capped after it is rounded down (damage order step 5) and before Guard absorbs it. The GDD's value; not in its "Tuning constants" table, which the owner may want to add it to. |
