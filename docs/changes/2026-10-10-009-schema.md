## Why

GDD "Statuses" and gap G6 (answered "accept"): a Grossed Out enemy "skips its next action", and its
intent is shown as skipped. The skip has to wait from the moment Ick crosses the threshold (usually
in the player phase) until the enemy's slot in the enemy phase, and it has to survive a snapshot.
No existing field holds it. Armless needs no new field, because `armless_used` plus a full set of
broken arms already says whether the skip is still waiting.

A count was chosen rather than a true/false flag. G6 says Ick applied while a skip is waiting
"still counts toward the new threshold", so a second Grossed Out before the enemy acts must not be
lost. It costs a second action. If the people decide one skip is the most, the core caps the count
at 1 and the field stays as it is.
