## Why

`tools/data_files.gd` needed no change: `read_all()` reads every folder under `data/`, and `core/ruleset/ruleset.gd` already knows `data/side_quests/` (task 025).

## Format notes
JSON has no comments, so a Side Quest file documents itself through its fields: `name` and `text` are what players read, each option's `label` is its button and its `text` states the option's effects exactly (the GDD's rules wording). Field meanings are in `core/schema/side_quest_def.gd`. Files use 2-space indentation and the key order of the fixture (`id`, `name`, `text`, `safe`, `dumb`; options `label`, `min_dolla_bills`, `text`, `effects`), like the other data files.
