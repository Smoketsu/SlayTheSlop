# 2026-10-11: Pile and deck viewers (task 043)

Author: Agent B. Branch: `b/043-pile-viewers`, stacked on `b/047-turn-feedback` (which stacks on
`b/045-tooltips`, #120 and #119). Lane: client (plus client tests). No core, data, schema,
project.godot or other contract change. Every pile it shows is already in the state
(`core/schema/dude_state.gd` `draw_pile`, `discard_pile`, `tapped_pile`, `deck`); nothing is
computed in the client.

## What changed

- **`client/visual/pile_viewer.gd`** (new): an overlay with a scrollable grid of card faces
  (`card_view.gd`) for a list of card copies, each from the copy's effective definition (a Lifted
  copy shows its upgrade and "+") with its keyword tooltip. `entries_for(ruleset, dude, uids,
  sorted)` (pure) builds the list, sorted by card type (`card_def.gd` TYPES order) then name then
  card_uid when asked; `show_cards(ruleset, title, note, entries)` draws it; `close()`, Escape, the
  Close button or a click outside the panel closes it (signal `closed`). It sends nothing and reads
  only its arguments.
- **`client/visual/card_view.gd`**: `hover_enabled` (false in a viewer: the face stays at rest).
- **`client/fight/fight_screen.gd`**: a left click on the draw, discard or Tapped pile icon (task
  057; counts already redrawn after every request) opens that pile's viewer: `open_pile(pile)`,
  `pile_viewer()`, `_on_pile_input()`. The draw pile is always shown sorted (`PILES_SORTED`), the
  others in pile order.
- **`client/run/run_screen.gd`**: a Deck button at the end of the header line, visible on the map
  and every offer screen (card reward, Boss Gear, The Tailgate, Baileys, Side Quest), hidden on the
  menu, in a fight and on the run-over view; `press_deck()` opens the whole `deck`, sorted, with
  upgrades; `deck_viewer()`.
- **`client/fight/messages.gd`**: `PILE_NOTES`. **`client/run/run_text.gd`**: `DECK_LABEL`,
  `DECK_NOTE`.
- **`tests/client/test_pile_viewers.gd`** (new).

## Why

Task 043: players could not see what was left in the draw pile, what they discarded, what was
Tapped, or their deck between fights.
