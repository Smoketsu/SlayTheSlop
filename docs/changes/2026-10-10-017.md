# 2026-10-10: Task 017, floor 1 enemy behaviors

Author: Agent A. Branch: `a/017-floor1-enemy-behaviors`. Lane: `core`.
The contract parts are their own commits and change files: `2026-10-10-017-constants.md`
(`FADED_MAX_DAMAGE`) and `2026-10-10-017-schema.md` (enemy definition fields, `haul`,
`drops_brews`, `brews_dropped`, seven events, status `faded`, `SCHEMA_VERSION` 6).

## What changed

Every floor 1 enemy in the GDD table can now be written as data and plays as described:

- **New files**
  - `core/combat/lineup.gd`: builds every enemy that joins a fight (moved out of `fight.gd`), and
    adds `arrives_with` escorts to the starting lineup.
  - `core/combat/enemy_behaviors.gd`: start checks, periodic statuses at turn start, `cards_played`
    and `unblocked_damage` triggers, and splitting.
  - `core/combat/enemy_death.gd`: after an enemy dies, its haul goes back and its Brew drop is reported.
  - `core/combat/periodic_statuses.gd`: periodic statuses and the Faded damage cap.
  - `core/ai/move_rules.gd`: move rules, tried before the pattern or random choice.
  - `core/ops/summon_enemy.gd`, `steal_dolla_bills.gd`, `flee.gd`, `gain_swole.gd`: four new ops.
- **Small, marked edits** ("Task 017") in `core/combat/fight.gd` (lineup, turn start, after each
  enemy effect and slot), `core/core.gd` (after a card; settle after every request),
  `core/combat/bodies.gd` (death hook), `core/ops/damage.gd` (Faded cap), `core/ai/intents.gd`
  (move rules first), `core/ops/ops.gd` (registry; `"ruleset"` in the context).
- `core/ops/README.md`: the four ops and a table of the enemy behavior fields.
- Tests: `tests/core/enemies/` (fixtures with the GDD's floor 1 enemies, one test per enemy),
  `tests/core/ai/test_move_rules.gd`, `tests/core/ops/test_enemy_ops.gd`.

GDD "Floor 1: The Basement" and task 017. Rules the GDD leaves open are decided as listed in task
