# 2026-10-13 - task 067: map nodes stay in the backdrop's open area

## What changed and why

Smoketsu saw the task 061 map nodes spill over the drawn rooms of the Basement backdrop. The nodes now
sit inside the picture's open middle area, measured on the picture, at any window size.

- `client/visual/map_art.gd`: `PLAY_AREAS` and `play_area()`.
- `client/run/map_screen.gd`: the board is placed over the open area as drawn (`play_rect`,
  `_play_rect()`), rows and columns spread to fit it, re-laid out on resize; the scroll container and
  `_keep_in_view()` are removed (the map always fits).
- `tests/client/test_map_visuals.gd`: nodes inside the area on every voting state at 1280x720 and on a
  new run at 1024x768 and 1920x1080; sizes compared approximately.

## Variables, fields and constants

Added in `client/visual/map_art.gd`: `PLAY_AREAS` - backdrop -> its open area for nodes, as fractions
(0..1) of the picture's width and height; changed only by the client lane after measuring a picture.

Added in `client/run/map_screen.gd` (changed only by the client lane):
- `HEADER_HEIGHT` - height of the header strip, in pixels (80); nodes start below it when a backdrop
  has no measured area.
- `DESIGN_SIZE` - the screen size assumed before the screen has one (1280x720).
- `play_rect` - where the nodes go, in screen pixels (read by tests).

Changed in `client/run/map_screen.gd`: `COLUMN_GAP` and `ROW_GAP` are now the most distance between
nodes (150 and 90 px); the actual gaps shrink to fit the area. `BOARD_MARGIN` is now used only when a
backdrop has no measured area.

Removed from `client/run/map_screen.gd`: `_scroll` (the scroll container) and `_keep_in_view()`.

No core field, constant or message was added, renamed or removed.
