## What changed

`data/constants.json` gets one new constant, `MAX_TRIGGER_DEPTH`. The change is additive: no
existing constant is renamed, removed or retuned. It sits after `BRO_METER_SOLO_CARDS` rather than
at the end of the file, so it does not touch the same lines as #46 (`FADED_MAX_DAMAGE`, appended
at the end) and the two merge in either order without a conflict.

## Why

`core/ops/ops.gd` fires Power triggers after every op, including the ops a trigger's own effects
run. A Power with `when: "enemy_buffed"` whose effects buff an enemy (Guard or Swole) would fire
itself forever and hang the fight. No slice card does this, but data could. Agent B raised it in
its review of #50.

Task 031's rules code (stacked on #50) counts how deeply triggers are nested and stops firing
them at this depth. A depth of 1 is not enough: Reply Guy's Ick (depth 1) can Gross Out an enemy,
which fires Friend Zone (depth 2). CLAUDE.md says "no magic numbers in code", so the cap is a
constant.

The GDD is silent on this rule. Task 031 adds it as gap G53 in
`docs/proposals/solo-slice-rule-gaps.md`, unanswered; 8 is a suggestion, not the GDD's number.

## Constants added

| Constant | Value | Unit | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MAX_TRIGGER_DEPTH` | 8 | nested Power triggers | Most Power triggers that may fire inside one another in one chain. A trigger fired by a card, enemy move or turn start runs its effects at depth 1; a trigger those effects set off runs at depth 2; no trigger fires from effects already at depth `MAX_TRIGGER_DEPTH`. An int, 1 or more; task 031 makes a fight refuse to start otherwise. Not in the GDD; the owner may want to add it to the "Tuning constants" table. |
