## Why

GDD "Goblin King Chad": "Keg Stand. His Pledges lift him and he charges for 2 turns, then hits one
Dude for 30. Each Pledge killed during the charge removes 10 damage. Kill both and he falls: he
skips a turn and is Exposed for 2." The charge lasts several turns and must survive a snapshot, so
it needs state; the fall's skip has to wait until his slot like Grossed Out's (G6), so it needs a
count. The numbers (2 turns, 2 Pledges, Exposed 2) are content, in the enemy file.

## Fields added, renamed or removed

### Enemy definition (`enemy_def.gd`), optional `charge`

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `charge.move_id` | text | The move shown while charging; picking it (normally by a move rule) starts the charge |
| `charge.turns` | int, 1 or more | Turns it charges, the first included (Keg Stand: 2) |
| `charge.release_move_id` | text | The move used the turn after the charge (Keg Stand's hit) |
| `charge.helper_def_id` | text | Definition of the enemies holding it up (`goblin_pledge`) |
| `charge.helpers` | int, 1 or more | How many hold it up (2); killing all of them makes it fall |
| `charge.fall_exposed` | int, 0 or more | Exposed stacks gained when it falls (2) |

### State (`enemy_state.gd`)

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `charge` | null or `{turns_charged, helper_uids, fall_exposed}` | The charge in progress. `turns_charged`: charge intents shown so far. `helper_uids`: the helpers chosen at the start. `fall_exposed`: copied from the definition |
| `fallen_skips` | int, 0 or more | Actions it will skip because it fell; each skipped action uses 1 (`enemy_skipped`, reason `fallen`) |

### Events (`event.gd`)

| Type | Fields | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `charge_started` | `enemy_uid`, `helper_uids` | An enemy started a charge |
| `enemy_fell` | `enemy_uid` | Every helper of its charge died; the charge is over |

| Constant | Where | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SCHEMA_VERSION` | `run_state.gd` | 6 -> 7 |

Nothing renamed or removed.
