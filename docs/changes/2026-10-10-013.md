# 2026-10-10: Task 013, Tom's card ops, Powers, pocketed, the solo Bro Meter and Chest Bump

Author: Agent A. Branch: `a/013-tom-card-ops-and-bro-meter`. Lane: `core`. The contract part
(`core/schema/`) is its own commit and its own change file, `2026-10-10-013-schema.md`.

## What changed

- **New ops** (`core/ops/`, registered in `ops.gd` `OPS`, documented in `core/ops/README.md`):
  `draw`, `gain_hype`, `self_damage` (names and fields as the lead fixed them), `guard_per_broken_arm`
  (Cast Party), `thorns` (Chrome Dome).
- **Generic effect fields** (`ops.gd`): any effect may carry `if` (a condition,
  `core/ops/conditions.gd`: `target_has_broken_arm`, `target_now_armless`) and `target`
  (`all_enemies`). `Ops.check()` validates them and passes the op's own check the rest.
- **Powers** (`core/combat/powers.gd`, `ops.gd` `fire_power_triggers_in_place`): a played card of
  type `power` goes to `dude.powers` and its `power_triggers` fire on `turn_start`,
  `enemy_buffed` (an enemy's positive `guard_changed`, G10) and `enemy_grossed_out`. Reactions are
  found from the events each op emits, right after the op, so they work wherever the buff or
  Grossed Out happens (cards, enemy moves, other triggers).
- **Thorns** (`thorns.gd`, `damage.gd`): each enemy hit on a Dude with thorns deals thorns damage
  back (G9, per hit; G3, not attack damage); an attacker killed by it stops attacking. Reset at Refresh.
- **Pocketed** (`piles.gd` `pocketed_in_hand`, `discard_hand_in_place` gains a `keep` parameter):
  pocketed cards stay in hand at End of turn. `tapped` already worked (task 007) and is tested again.
- **Bro Meter** (`core/combat/bro_meter.gd`): solo +1 on every `BRO_METER_SOLO_CARDS`-th card in
  a turn, capped at `BRO_METER_MAX`, `bro_meter_changed`. Co-op rule not built (documented stub:
  in co-op the meter never gains yet). `Fight.check_start` now refuses a fight whose two Bro Meter
  constants are missing.
- **use_bro_move** (`core/combat/bro_move.gd`, `core/core.gd`): `not_ready` below max; at max
  the meter empties and the Dude's `bro_move` card is played for free (no Hype, not from the hand,
  not counted as a card played), as attack damage. `unplayable` if the card or its ops are missing,
  `bad_target` for a bad optional `target_id`.
- **guard.gd**: the guard rule moved into `gain_in_place()` so `guard_per_broken_arm` shares it.
  `run()` behaves exactly as before.
- **damage.gd**: thorns, and stopping the effect when its attacker dies part way.
- **fight.gd / play.gd / piles.gd / core.gd**: small calls marked "Task 013" (thorns reset,
  turn-start triggers, pocketed, Bro Meter, Power destination, Bro Move request).
- **Tests** (`tests/core/ops/`): `tom_fixtures.gd` (in-memory copies of Tom's 14 GDD cards, his Bro
  Move, two keyword cards, Dudes `test_tom` and `test_tom_all`), `test_tom_cards.gd` (one test per
  card plus Powers, pocketed/tapped, clearing at the next fight), `test_bro_meter.gd`,
  `test_task013_ops.gd`, `test_tom_fixed_seed_fight.gd`. Updated: `tests/core/combat/test_core_apply.gd`
  (`use_bro_move` at max now applies), `test_fight_flow.gd` (third card of a turn emits
  `bro_meter_changed`).
- **Re-recorded hashes**: `FINAL_HASH` in `tests/core/combat/test_fixed_seed_fight.gd` and
  `test_fixed_seed_status_fight.gd`. The solo Bro Meter now fills during those fights, so the final
  `bro_meter` differs. With the Bro Meter gain switched off by hand, both hashes matched the
  contract commit's values exactly, so nothing else changed. Every outcome assertion still holds.
