# 2026-10-10: Task 019, Goblin King Chad

Author: Agent A. Branch: `a/019-goblin-king-chad` (on top of task 017). Lane: `core`.
The contract part is its own commit and change file: `2026-10-10-019-schema.md` (enemy `charge`,
`fallen_skips`, `charge_started`, `enemy_fell`, `SCHEMA_VERSION` 7).

## What changed

- **New files**
  - `core/ai/charge.gd`: a charge in progress decides the enemy's intent; picking the charge's
    move starts it (`charge_started`); counts helpers lost.
  - `core/ops/charged_hit.gd`: the op for the charge's release (base damage minus a cut per helper
    lost, then an ordinary attack hit), which ends the charge.
- **Edits**
  - `core/ai/intents.gd`: a charge first, then move rules, then pattern or random; start a charge
    after `intent_set`.
  - `core/combat/enemy_death.gd`: the fall when the last helper of a charge dies (`enemy_fell`,
    Exposed, `fallen_skips`).
  - `core/combat/statuses.gd`: skip reason `fallen` (after Grossed Out, before Armless).
  - `core/ops/ops.gd`: registers `charged_hit`. `core/ops/README.md`: `charged_hit` and `charge`.
- Tests: `tests/core/enemies/test_goblin_king_chad.gd` (one test per acceptance check and a
  fixed-seed scripted fight with a recorded hash); Chad, a Haymaker card and a test Dude in
  `tests/core/enemies/enemy_fixtures.gd`.
