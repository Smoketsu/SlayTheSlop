# 2026-10-10: Proposal for 16 more Tom cards

## What changed

- Added `docs/proposals/tom-milestone2-cards.md`: a draft of 16 new Tom cards (6 common,
  6 uncommon, 4 rare) that brings Tom's pool to the 30 cards GDD milestone 2 asks for.
  It also lists the new `core` rules six of them would need and the open decisions.

## Why

The GDD designs 14 Tom cards. Milestone 2 needs about 30. Agents may not invent cards, so they go
to both people as a proposal.

## Fields, variables and constants

- None added, renamed or removed. The proposal suggests one future constant,
  `NOTHING_TO_LOSE_CAP` (30), which exists only if the card is accepted.
