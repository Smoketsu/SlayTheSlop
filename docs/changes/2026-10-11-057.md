# 2026-10-11: Fight layout and stat icons (task 057)

Author: Agent B. Branch: `b/057-fight-layout`, stacked on task 041 (`b/041-card-faces`, PR #112).
Lane: client (plus client tests and placeholder assets). No core, data, schema or contract change;
nothing is added to game state. The client still only reads state: the attack number and the
"will skip" icon come from read-only core functions (`core/ops/damage.gd` `hit_amount()`,
`core/combat/statuses.gd` `intended_move()` and `skip_reason()`), not from rules copied into the
client.

## What changed

- **`client/visual/backgrounds.gd`**: a floor-line table next to the room table. Measured on each
  picture at 1280x720: floor back edge 0.55 (Basement), 0.54 (Goblin King Chad's room), 0.55 (Rat
  King's lair); feet set at 0.60 in each, a little in front of the back edge.
- **`client/visual/floor_layout.gd`** (new): a Container that stands every combatant view on the
  floor line, Dudes on the left, enemies on the right, evenly spaced in state order, one slot width
  for both sides (shrinking when crowded), everything under the feet above the hand area. Pure slot
  plan in `slots()`.
- **`client/visual/combatant_view.gd`** (rewritten layout, same animation API): no panel behind the
  character (a faint light on hover only); intent icon over the head; sprite fitted to its art's
  aspect with its bottom edge on the floor line; name; HP bar with "current / max" inside and a heart
  at its left end; one row of badges, each only when its value is not 0, shrunk to stay one row.
  In co-op a Dude shows a seat marker in its seat colour, and an attack shows one marker per targeted
  Dude in that Dude's colour. A Passed Out Dude now tips over about its feet.
- **`client/visual/stat_badge.gd`** (new): one icon with a number (beside it, or on top of it for the
  Hype orb), tooltip "<name>: <value>", clicks pass through; a key with no icon shows a short text tag.
- **`client/visual/icons.gd`** (new): the one icon table, keyed by GDD field name, status id, intent
  kind or client key, with `look()`, `has_icon()`, `short_text()`, `icon_path()`.
- **`client/fight/fight_screen.gd`**: the floor (`floor_layout.gd`) replaces the Dude and enemy
  boxes; the top strip holds the turn label ("Turn 1 - player phase", no Bro Meter), the message and
  the event log; the bottom `HAND_SHARE` holds the Hype orb and draw pile (left), the hand, and the
  Bro Meter bar, the buttons and the discard and Tapped piles (right). The room shade is lighter.
- **`client/fight/fight_model.gd`**: additive fields for the icons (see below); existing fields
  unchanged.
- **`client/fight/messages.gd`**: `SKIP_REASONS`, words for why an enemy will skip its move.
- **`assets/placeholder/icons/`** (new): 26 SVG icons drawn for this repository, with their Godot
  `.import` files. **`assets/placeholder/source/make_icons.py`** (new) writes them.
  **`assets/placeholder/README.md`**: new "Icons" section recording each file.
- **`tests/client/test_fight_layout.gd`** (new, 12 tests), see the task file's log.
- **`docs/tasks/057-fight-layout-and-stat-icons.md`**: checks ticked, log entry, `status: in_review`.

## Why

Smoketsu's feedback on the fight screen at 1280x720: characters floated at the top in panels, and
the text under them was too wordy (task 057 "Goal").
