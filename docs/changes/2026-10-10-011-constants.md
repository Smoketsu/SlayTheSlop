## What changed

`data/constants.json` gets one new constant, `MULTI_TARGET_COUNT`. The change is additive:
no existing constant is renamed, removed or retuned.

## Why

GDD "Difficulty scaling" says how many Dudes a single-target enemy attack hits: 1 at party
sizes 1 and 2, and 2 at sizes 3 and 4. `MULTI_TARGET_PARTY_SIZE` (3) already holds the party size
at which this changes. The count itself, 2, was not stored anywhere. CLAUDE.md says "no magic
numbers in code", so task 011 reads the count from the constants instead of writing 2 in
`core/ai/`.

## Constants added

| Constant | Value | Unit | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MULTI_TARGET_COUNT` | 2 | Dudes | How many different Dudes a single-target enemy attack hits once the party has `MULTI_TARGET_PARTY_SIZE` players. Below that party size it hits 1. If fewer Dudes are standing, it hits all of them. This is the GDD's value and is not tuned. The owner may want to add it to the GDD's "Tuning constants" table. |
