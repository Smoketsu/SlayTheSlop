# 2026-10-11: Task 035, cards that target a Dude ("ally"), and Guard that lands on them (Bro Hug)

Author: Agent A. Branch: `a/035-ally-targets`. Core lane; no contract, data or state change.

## What changed

- **`core/combat/play.gd`**: `targets()` accepts `target: "ally"` with the `player_id` of any Dude
  who is not Passed Out, the player's own included. For an ally card, the op context's
  `GuardOp.ON_TARGETS_KEY` is true. Header comment updated.
- **`core/ops/guard.gd`**: with `ON_TARGETS_KEY`, each target gains the Guard instead of the actor.
  `gain_in_place` takes an optional `receiver_id`: the giver's Chill applies and the receiver's
  Hungover. Unchanged everywhere else.
- **`core/ops/README.md`**: "Who an effect lands on" and `guard` describe ally cards.
- **`docs/proposals/solo-slice-rule-gaps.md`**: new gap G62 (who may be targeted, whose Chill and
  Hungover), built as suggested.
- **`tests/core/ops/test_ally_targets.gd`** (new, 5 tests); **`tests/core/combat/test_core_apply.gd`**:
  the old "ally is always bad_target" check now checks no target and an enemy.
- **`docs/tasks/035-ally-targets.md`**: checks ticked, log, `in_review`.

## Why

Bro Hug (`tom_bro_hug`), one of the 16 new Tom cards both people accepted (#91), is the first Assist
card: "Target Dude gains 7 Guard". Cards with `target: "ally"` were refused, and `guard` always
landed on the player.
