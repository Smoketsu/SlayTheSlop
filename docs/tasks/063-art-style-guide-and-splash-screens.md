## Goal

Smoketsu (2026-10-12): Side Quests and Baileys need splash screens that "both evoke humor and show
what's going on. Without this, the game simply won't feel right." He also wants a sprite guide in
general, so assets stay consistent from now on, including small animations.

Do it in two parts:

1. **A style guide** (`assets/STYLE_GUIDE.md`) so every future asset matches what exists. It covers:
   - **Palette, outline and shading:** read from the current character sheet and backgrounds.
   - **Sizes:** character sprite sizes and canvas, the floor line (task 057), splash canvas size and
     safe area for UI, and icon size.
   - **Poses and expressions per Dude:** Tom first, then the others as they arrive.
   - **Naming:** files are named by content id (as the sprite lookup already does).
   - **Animation conventions:** frame counts and frame rate for idle, attack, hit and Passed Out, and
     how frames are packed (sprite sheet layout).
   - **Prompts:** a reusable prompt template for image generation, so new art comes out in the same
     style. Smoketsu's existing art came from ChatGPT image generation (see `assets/placeholder/README.md`).
   - **Sources and rules:** how every new file's source is recorded, and the CLAUDE.md rules (no real
     brands, logos or likenesses; Baileys sells Shake, not drinks).
2. **Splash screens** shown when the party enters a node:
   - one per Side Quest (5, `data/side_quests/`);
   - one for Baileys, with the shop inside it: the counter, the guy behind it, Shake on the shelf;
   - one for The Tailgate.

   Each splash shows the scene and the joke at a glance. The choices and buttons sit on top of it, in
   a safe area the guide defines.
